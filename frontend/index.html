Objective

One platform, from raw sensor data to conservation action.

The system uses image recognition, acoustic analysis, computer vision, and machine learning to automatically identify wildlife species, estimate population sizes, monitor biodiversity changes, detect endangered species, and analyze habitat health.

It ingests camera trap images, drone imagery, satellite data, environmental sensors, and wildlife audio recordings to support biodiversity conservation, ecological monitoring, habitat management, and wildlife research — replacing manual review with continuous, automated intelligence.

Every model output — a detection, a call, a population trend — is designed to resolve into one thing: a clear, actionable signal for the person deciding where to send a patrol or where to focus restoration.

  • GPS, habitat type, protected area logging

Wildlife Image Analysis Engine

  • Camera trap & drone image processing
  • Animal detection + bounding boxes
  • Individual animal identification
MOD-04AUDIO

Bioacoustic Recognition Engine

  • Bird, mammal, amphibian & insect calls
  • Acoustic event detection
  • Environmental noise filtering
MOD-05SPECIES

Species Identification Engine

  • Taxonomic classification + confidence scoring
  • Endangered species detection
  • Unknown species flagging
MOD-06POP

Population Estimation Engine

  • Counting, density & trend analysis

  • Species distribution mapping
MOD-07BIODIV

Biodiversity Intelligence Engine

Habitat Intelligence Engine
